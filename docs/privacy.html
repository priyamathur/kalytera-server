Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Kalytera checks the conversations and actions of AI agents for the businesses that use it. This page explains what personal information we collect, why, who it goes to, and how to delete it. Questions: info@kalytera.dev.

Information about you

  • Your account: the name or company and the email address you sign up with.
  • Signing in with Google: Google tells us your email address (and your name and profile picture, which we don't keep). We use the email only to find your Kalytera account. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; we keep the Stripe customer and subscription IDs to know your plan.
  • Cookies: the dashboard sets a cookie to keep you signed in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Information your agents send us

When you connect Langfuse or LangSmith, or add Kalytera to your agent, we receive your agents' conversations: what was said, the actions taken and what they returned. These can contain personal information about your own customers. We process it only to provide Kalytera to you: judging conversations, finding problems, alerting you and checking rules. For this data you are the controller and we process it on your behalf.

You can mask email addresses, phone numbers and card numbers before they leave your servers (kalytera.init(redact=True)). Details in Data & security.

Who we share it with

We don't sell personal information or use it for advertising. It goes only to the services that run Kalytera:

  • Render (servers) and Neon (database), in the United States
  • Anthropic, whose Claude model judges each finished conversation
  • Stripe for payments, Google for sign-in, and Slack if you connect alerts

Anonymous pattern sharing (on by default, off any time in the dashboard) shares only aggregated, anonymous patterns between companies: kinds of mistakes and the shapes of rules. Never conversation text, names or anything that identifies you or your customers.

We'll disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Conversations and their results are kept until you delete them, or for the retention period you choose (30, 90, 180 or 365 days). Your account details are kept while your account is open.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete an agent's data yourself in the dashboard (Overview → Delete this agent's data).
  • To see, correct, export or delete your account and all its data, email info@kalytera.dev. We'll respond within 30 days.
  • If your company's customers ask about their data, contact us and we'll help you answer.

Security

API keys are stored only as hashes, and connection keys and Slack webhooks are encrypted. Every request is scoped to your account. More in Data & security.

Children

Kalytera is a business service and isn't meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, email account owners.
